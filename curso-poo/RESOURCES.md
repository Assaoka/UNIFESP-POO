- **Dijkstra, Edsger W. (1968)**: *"Go To Statement Considered Harmful"*. Communications of the ACM.
- **Kay, Alan (1993)**: *"The Early History of Smalltalk"*. ACM SIGPLAN Notices.
- **Beck, Kent (2002)**: *"Test Driven Development: By Example"*. Addison-Wesley.
- **TypeScript Handbook**: [TypeScript Official Documentation](https://www.typescriptlang.org/docs/handbook/intro.html).
